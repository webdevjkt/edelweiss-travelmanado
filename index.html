Explore The Beauty Of Bunaken

View Tour:

Bunaken is an island of 8.08 km² in the Bay of Manado, located north of Sulawesi. Reachable by 30-minute speedboat from Manado port. The Bunaken marine park spans 75,265 hectares across 5 islands (Bunaken, Manado Tua, Siladen, Mantehage, Naen) and features world-class coral reefs, sea turtles, scuba diving, and dolphin watching.

Magical Of Tangkoko National Park

View Tour:

Tangkoko National Park in Bitung is an extraordinary nature destination, home to 47 protected endemic species. Encounter the world's smallest nocturnal primate, the Tarsier (Tarsius spectrum) with its glowing eyes, and the Black Crested Macaque, finished with sunset relaxation at Batu Putih Beach.

FAQ's

Frequently Asked
Questions

Have questions? You're in the right place! This section provides quick answers to some of the most common inquiries we receive about our tours.

1. How Do I Book A Tour?

You can book your tour directly via WhatsApp, our website booking form, or email. We're happy to assist you personally with recommendations and availability.

2. Are Your Tours Private Or Shared?

All of our tours are 100% private. You will have your own modern air-conditioned vehicle and professional English-speaking driver guide — no sharing with strangers.

3. What Is Included In The Tour Package?

Our standard tours include private air-conditioned vehicle, friendly English-speaking local driver/guide, fuel/petrol, parking fees, attraction entrance tickets, local lunch, and mineral water.

4. Can I Customize My Tour Itinerary?

Absolutely! 100% customized itineraries are our specialty. Whether you want to visit specific photo spots in Tomohon, snorkel longer in Bunaken, or explore Tangkoko wildlife, we will tailor the trip to your schedule.

5. Do You Offer Airport Transfers?

Yes, we provide 24/7 reliable airport pickup and drop-off services from/to Sam Ratulangi International Airport (MDC) to Manado City, Tomohon, Bitung, Likupang resorts, or Bunaken ferry harbors.
